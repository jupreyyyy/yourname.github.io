SHEET 02 — ABOUT

About Geoffrey

Geoffrey is a Civil Engineering Technology graduate with hands-on experience in construction estimating and virtual assistance. His background covers quantity takeoffs, Bill of Quantities preparation, and technical drafting in AutoCAD 3D, alongside day-to-day administrative work — data management, document organization, graphic design, and social media support.

He works best on tasks that reward precision: reading drawings closely, checking numbers twice, and keeping files organized so nothing gets lost between the field and the office. Detail-oriented, organized, and committed to accuracy on every deliverable.
